AI ORCHESTRATION · PLATFORM DESIGN

OMNIX — AI Command Center

Problem: Organizations experimenting with multiple AI tools need a way to understand costs, coordinate requests, and introduce governance before allowing automated actions.

Solution: A Next.js and FastAPI foundation with SQLite history, model tiers, usage tracking, and an advisory approval queue. The repository explicitly identifies task execution and external integrations as unfinished.

Workplace application: A mature version could give IT and operations leaders one controlled interface for routing AI requests, tracking consumption, and requiring human approval for sensitive actions.

Potential benefit, not a measured outcome: better AI cost visibility and stronger human oversight.

HEALTH OPERATIONS · WORKFLOW DESIGN

Au-Some Teacher OS — Practice Platform

Problem: Clinical practices often coordinate scheduling, treatment programs, staff assignments, and reporting across separate tools, increasing administrative friction.

Solution: A developing configurable workspace centered on clinical workflows, treatment planning, progress visualization, and role-aware access. The preview uses fictional data and does not imply HIPAA readiness.

Workplace application: In a validated implementation, centralized workflows could reduce duplicate entry, make review processes more consistent, and help managers identify operational bottlenecks.

Potential benefit, not a measured outcome: less administrative duplication and more consistent reporting.

KNOWLEDGE WORK · AUTOMATION

AI Publishing Engine — Quality-Controlled Content

Problem: Research-heavy content production requires repeatable steps for source gathering, drafting, consistency checks, and editorial sign-off.

Solution: An iterative Google Apps Script workflow intended to separate research, writing, historical validation, and human editorial review rather than treating one AI response as a finished deliverable.

Workplace application: The same pattern can support policy drafts, training materials, internal knowledge bases, and communications teams where traceability and human review matter.

Potential benefit, not a measured outcome: reusable content workflows and clearer quality checkpoints.
